# Reham portfolio

Selected source: [Webflow-inspired DESIGN.md](https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md/blob/main/design-md/webflow/DESIGN.md) from VoltAgent/awesome-design-md. Adapted, not a Webflow clone.

Blue #1458dd; gray canvas #f2f3f5; ink #24282f; secondary text #626975; white editor surfaces. Dark theme uses the same family inverted: ground #111318, surface #1b1e24, ink #f2f3f5, muted #a0a6b1. Brand blue stays #1458dd for fills and display type in both themes; small accent text on dark uses --accent-text #8ab0f7 because the brand blue only reaches 3:1 there. Fraunces with a Georgia fallback, tight display tracking, restrained weights. Full-width personal wordmark, compact navigation, generous spacing, precise rectangular controls. Four original code-drawn concept covers, not representations of client commissions.

Idea: Reham's name becomes the central typographic object, and it is a toy. The letters of the name are physical: they drop into the hero one by one and bounce, and each landing rings through a precise dot grid where every dot is a small spring. Visitors can grab a letter, throw it around the hero, or tap it to pop it into the air. Flying letters bounce off the walls, knock loose the letters they hit and shoulder the dots aside; a tap on empty space sends out a ripple that lifts the letters nearby. Left alone for a few seconds, they hop home and spell the name again, and the full stop bounces now and then to show it is alive. A finger or cursor drags a wake through the grid, even while scrolling, an unseen wake wanders when nothing is touching the page, and scrolling shakes the field and pushes the currents along. The glow is held back behind the copy. The Motion button sends the letters home and eases the grid flat. Work filters and project dialogs allow visitors to explore the sample projects.

Motion: GSAP ScrollTrigger with Lenis smooth scroll (src/motion.ts). The hero's copy clears as the work section slides over it, and the top of that sheet carries the letters up and out of the hero, wobbling on their springs; project cards stack and recede on desktop and tilt and scale in with the scroll on phones; the two about lines pass each other and the statement inks in word by word; the contact panel opens from an inset card to full bleed into the blue footer, where the name returns in white in both themes. Touch has no hover, so on phones the service rows light up as they cross the middle of the screen and the contact arrow turns with the scroll. All of it sits on top of complete CSS, so the page is whole with reduced motion or JavaScript off. Theme: follows the system until a choice is saved (src/theme.ts). Switching to light pours the page out of the toggle; switching to dark plays that backwards, drawing the light back in. The toggle is its own layer during the wipe, so its icon spins above the page and the wipe's centre comes from where the browser placed that layer, which keeps it on the button when a phone's URL bar is showing.

Feedback: the loader is the hero's dot grid in miniature, a ripple leaving the centre of nine dots. Toasts and the confirm dialog sit on the same surface, line and shadow tokens as the rest of the page, and stay inside the blue and gray family; only errors and destructive actions use danger red. Usage rules are in AGENTS.md.

Section argument: hero introduces the person and discipline; selected work demonstrates sample visual directions; about describes the approach; contact provides a direct email action. Admin supports editing these fields and adding/removing projects.

Skill direction: name-as-monument. Deviations: no portrait, project photographs, or video were supplied, so no stock person is presented as Reham. Original concept artwork replaces photographic previews. The user's requested subtle particles replace the dealt shutter motion. The chosen repository design and blue/gray brief govern tokens.

Sample content to replace: role, biography, email, project titles/descriptions/years and artwork. No invented clients, metrics, testimonials, or professional achievements.
